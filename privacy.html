Privacy Policy

Bonsai · Last updated 25 July 2026

The short version: Bonsai collects nothing about you. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you log stays on your device. Honesty is the whole point of the app — and that starts with your data.

Information we collect

None. Bonsai does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, or any identifiers.

What stays on your device

The app saves your progress — your tree’s health and growth, your logged actions, and your settings — locally on your device only, using the operating system’s standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your phone, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is removed if you delete the app.

No tracking, no ads, no third parties

This version of Bonsai works fully offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party services that could receive your information. Nothing you do in the app is verified, monitored, or reported anywhere.

Children

Bonsai does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is safe for all ages in that regard.

Changes to this policy

If future versions of Bonsai add optional features that involve third parties (for example, ads or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated before those features ship, and this page will describe exactly what changes. The date above always reflects the current version.
