Privacy Policy

Bonsai · Last updated 30 July 2026

The short version: Bonsai is free and kept free by ads. Everything you log — your actions, your tree’s health and growth — stays on your device; we never upload it, sell it, or verify it. To show ads and handle optional purchases we rely on two well-known services, Google AdMob and RevenueCat, described below. That’s the complete picture.

What stays on your device

Your habit data — logged actions, hearts, growth, keepsakes, and settings — is saved only on your device using standard on-device storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is removed if you delete the app. There is no account and no sign-in.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Because Bonsai is free, it shows ads through Google AdMob — an ad when you open the app, and optional “rewarded” ads you can choose to watch to revive a tree. To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect and process device information such as a mobile advertising identifier, IP address (and coarse location derived from it), device type, and ad interactions.

On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking when prompted (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you’ll still see ads, but non-personalized ones. See how Google uses this data: Google Ads & Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

Optional purchases — removing ads (monthly or lifetime) and instant tree revival — are billed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed for us by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random app-user identifier and your purchase status so your purchase unlocks correctly (and restores on a new device). Your payment details go directly to Apple or Google — we never see or store your card information. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

What we never do

No accounts, no personal profiles, no uploading or selling your habit logs, and no verification or monitoring of what you do in real life. The honesty in Bonsai is between you and yourself.

Your choices

  • Remove ads entirely with the in-app “Remove ads” subscription or one-time lifetime purchase.
  • Limit ad personalization via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Android Settings → Google → Ads (reset or delete your advertising ID).

Children

Bonsai is not directed to children under 13, and where required, ads are requested in a non-personalized mode.

Changes to this policy

If we change what data is involved, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date above before the change ships.
